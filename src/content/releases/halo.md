HALO is the album the whole era was building towards. SAME LA, PRETTY4U, BREAKUP, TELL ME WHAT I DID, START OVER and AMERICAN GIRL all came out first, one by one, and on 3 April 2026 they finally found their place among thirteen songs. For us it is the HALO era in one record, from the first single to the last track.
