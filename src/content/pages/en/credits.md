## The artist

Everything this site celebrates is the work of **Tiffany Day** and the people she makes it with. Listen on her official channels, starting at [tiffdidwhat.com](https://www.tiffdidwhat.com/).

## Covers and photos

Release covers come from the [Cover Art Archive](https://coverartarchive.org/) and are shown with written permission. Photos of the artist appear with written permission, each with its photographer's credit next to it.

## Data

- Discography: [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/), released under CC0. Thank you to its volunteer editors.
- Tour dates: her official tour page.
- The name "Shroomies" comes from the fan community.
