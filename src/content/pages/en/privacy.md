## Who is responsible

The data controller is **swogvex**, who runs this site from Spain. Contact: [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com).

## What stays in your browser

Some choices are saved in your own browser (`localStorage`), never in a cookie and never sent to us:

- your theme (Original, Light or Dark) and calm mode;
- the songs *Surprise me* has already sent you to, so it doesn't repeat them;
- later on: the stickers you find, your streak and your progress.

You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. Because they only make the features you use work, no consent banner is needed. **We don't use cookies.**

## What our host sees

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it processes your IP address and basic browser details to deliver the pages and to protect the site from abuse. We don't use this to identify you, and we don't run analytics, ads or profiling.

## Spotify and YouTube players

Players never load on their own. Only when you press the play button does the official Spotify or YouTube player load; from then on those services may set their own cookies and collect data under their own privacy policies.

## The fan wall (opening soon)

When the wall opens, this is what happens with your note:

- **Stored:** the note text, an optional nickname, an optional country, the note style and the language.
- **Never asked:** email, real name, age or an account.
- **Your IP address** is only used in a scrambled form that changes every day, to stop spam and floods, and it is deleted after 24 hours.
- A scrambled **device code** lets each device react to or report a note only once. It is kept while the note exists.
- Your **delete code** is stored scrambled, so only you can use it.

Legal basis: your consent when you send a note (GDPR art. 6.1.a) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse (art. 6.1.f).

How long: approved notes stay until you delete them or ask us to; rejected notes are deleted after 30 days; pending notes stay until they are reviewed.

Please don't write personal data in a note, yours or anyone's. Every note is read before it appears.

## Who helps us run it

- **Vercel Inc.**: hosting, in the United States, with the Standard Contractual Clauses the GDPR requires.
- **Neon**: the wall's database, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).
- **Resend**: a weekly email to the owner with numbers only, never the text of a note.
- **GitHub**: stores the site's code; no visitor data.

## Under 14?

The site doesn't ask your age because it doesn't collect personal data. In Spain, people under 14 need a parent's consent to share personal data online, so please don't share any on the wall.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, and you can withdraw your consent at any time: write to [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com). Wall notes can also be deleted directly with their delete code. If you think we got something wrong, you can complain to the Spanish data protection authority, the [AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/).

## Changes

If anything here changes, we update this page and its date. If we ever wanted to add something that needs your consent, we would ask first.
