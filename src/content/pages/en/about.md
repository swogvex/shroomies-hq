## What this is

Shroomies HQ is a home for Tiffany Day fans: her whole discography era by era, a tracker that counts the days between releases, a journal of news, her rooms through the eras and, soon, a wall of notes for Tiff. Pick a track on the remote and press play.

## Who made it

It is made by **swogvex**, a fan, in their free time. It is a personal, non-commercial project: no ads, no sales and no tracking.

## Unofficial

Shroomies HQ is not affiliated with or endorsed by Tiffany Day, Broke Records or her team. For anything official, go to her channels:
