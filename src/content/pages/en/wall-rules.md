## Before you pin a note

- **Be kind.** Notes are for Tiff and for the fans.
- **No personal data,** yours or anyone else's: no names of private people, emails, phone numbers, @handles or links.
- **No hate,** insults, harassment or sexual content.
- **No attacks on other artists.**
- **No spam or ads.**

## How notes are checked

Every note is read by a person before it appears, about **once a week**, so yours may take a few days. A filter stops notes with contact details or insults before they are sent and tells you why, so you can fix them.

## Reports

If a note breaks the rules, use **⋯ → Report**. Each device can report a note once. A note with three reports is hidden until it is reviewed.

## Deleting your note

When you pin a note you get a **delete code**. It is the only way to delete the note yourself (there is no email and no account), so keep it somewhere safe. You can also write to [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com).

## We may remove notes

We may remove any note that breaks these rules or that we are unsure about. How your data is handled: [privacy](/privacy/).
