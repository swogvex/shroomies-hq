## Who runs this site

Shroomies HQ (shroomieshq.fan) is run by **swogvex**, a private person living in Spain, as a personal and non-commercial project. The site has no ads, sells nothing and takes no donations.

Contact: [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com). Messages are read by the person who runs the site, usually within a week.

## An unofficial fan site

Shroomies HQ is made by fans. It is **not official** and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Tiffany Day, Broke Records or her team. Her name is used only to say who the site is about. For official news, go to [her official site](https://www.tiffdidwhat.com/).

## Using the site

You are welcome to browse and to share links and screenshots. Please don't copy the site's design, code or texts into other projects without asking first. The fan wall has [its own rules](/wall-rules/).

## Links to other sites

We link to official channels and licensed sources (for example, for lyrics). We check each link when we add it, but we are not responsible for what other sites publish or change later.

## Accuracy

Everything here is collected with care from public, official sources, but mistakes happen. If you spot one, tell us and we will fix it. The site may change or go offline at any time.
